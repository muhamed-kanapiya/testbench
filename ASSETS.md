# Illustrative asset set

Created using the built-in imagegen tool for TestBench. This is a fictional skincare product used to demonstrate a controlled creative experiment. It is not a real customer, campaign or performance claim.

Project paths:

- `public/assets/product-original.png`: original generated transparent product image.
- `public/assets/product.png`: the same product with transparent outer padding trimmed for composition.
- `public/assets/background-studio.png`: lavender studio example.
- `public/assets/background-home.png`: blue bathroom shelf example.
- `public/assets/background-nature.png`: peach stone botanical example.

## Product prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium photorealistic product cutout for an illustrative advertising experiment website. One upright compact cylindrical aluminium skincare bottle with rounded shoulders and a matte deep cobalt blue cap. Satin warm-silver body. A striking burnt-orange rectangular paper label on its front, printed dark navy lowercase brand text exactly 'morrow' in a large beautiful geometric sans-serif; smaller text 'daily ritual' and '250 ml'. Three-quarter view very slightly from above, primarily front facing, label perfectly readable. Full bottle completely in frame with generous transparent padding around it; centred; bottle occupies 82% of image height. Straight vertical bottle, no tilting. Soft diffused professional studio key light from upper left; authentic fine metallic texture; luxury contemporary skincare art direction. TRANSPARENT alpha background, no ground, no pedestal, no cast shadow, no extra objects, no watermark. High resolution portrait-square asset. This is a fictional demonstration brand.

Strong visual simplicity, cool muted lavender palette, nuanced real photographic materials. Completely empty centre where a product will be composited later at 60% height. Top quarter is uncluttered pale lilac negative space for dark advertising typography. Camera square-on with slight view of plinth top. No product, no bottle, no label, no text, no logos, no people, no watermark. Premium contemporary art direction, physically plausible soft light from upper left.
